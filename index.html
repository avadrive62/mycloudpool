MyCloudPoolPrivacy policy

Personal file management

One place to work with my cloud files.

MyCloudPool is the project name for a private setup that uses rclone on Windows and RSAF on Android to manage the owner's authorized Google Drive accounts.

It is for the owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public storage or account-registration service.

What the setup does

  • Lists and opens files and folders in the accounts the owner authorizes.
  • Uploads, downloads, organizes, renames and deletes files at the owner's direction.
  • Presents selected folders together through an rclone Union configuration and places new files according to its storage rule.

Each Google account retains its own ownership, permissions and storage quota. This project does not increase an account's Google Drive quota.

Access to Google Drive

Google authorization is requested by the locally installed clients, using the owner's Google Cloud OAuth configuration. File-management access is used to perform the operations described above.

Connection credentials are kept by those clients on the owner's devices. Files remain in the connected Google Drive accounts, with local copies or caches when the owner opens, downloads or edits them.

This website only explains the personal setup and its privacy practices. It contains no Google sign-in form and does not receive Google Drive files or account credentials.

Privacy and control

Read the MyCloudPool privacy policy for the data used by this setup, storage practices and how to revoke access.

For questions, use the support email listed on this project's Google authorization screen.
